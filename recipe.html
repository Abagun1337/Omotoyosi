Classic Spaghetti Carbonara

This Italian pasta dish is a timeless classic, made with creamy eggs, savory cheese, crispy pancetta, and freshly ground black pepper. Simple, quick, and absolutely delicious!

Recipe Information

Preparation Time: 15 minutes

Cooking Time: 20 minutes

Servings:4

Difficulty:Intermediate

Ingredients

  • 400g (14 oz) spaghetti
  • 150g (5 oz) pancetta or guanciale, diced
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • Salt (to taste)

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until al dente.
  2. Meanwhile, heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the pancetta until crispy.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, yolk, and grated Parmesan cheese until creamy.
  4. Drain the pasta, reserving 1/2 cup of the cooking water.
  5. Toss the pasta with pancetta, then remove from heat and stir in the egg-cheese mixture. Add reserved water if needed to make it creamy.
  6. Season generously with black pepper and serve immediately.

Tips

For best results, do not overcook the pasta. Work quickly when adding the egg mixture to prevent scrambling. You can also substitute Parmesan with Pecorino Romano for a sharper flavor.
